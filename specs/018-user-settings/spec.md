# Feature Specification: User Settings

**Feature Branch**: `018-user-settings`

**Created**: 2026-07-23

**Status**: Draft

**Input**: User description: "User Settings for RiskJS — a settings page for a signed-in player to view/change their account display name, any notification/default-game-option preferences, sign out, and delete their account (a deliberate, hard-to-trigger-by-accident, irreversible action). Depends on the Account System (006) for the underlying identity; doesn't redefine auth or the account model. Excludes admin-side account management and the separate device-level guest username (005)."

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - View and change my account display name (Priority: P1)

A signed-in player can see their current account display name and change it
to something else, with the new name reflected wherever their account
identity is shown (stats, friends list, etc.).

**Why this priority**: The most basic, expected capability of any account
settings surface — a player needs to be able to see and correct how they're
identified before anything else here matters.

**Independent Test**: As a signed-in player, open settings, change the
display name, and confirm the new name is shown consistently afterward
wherever the account's identity appears.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a signed-in player opens their settings, **When** the page
   loads, **Then** they see their current account display name.
2. **Given** a signed-in player changes their display name to a new valid
   value, **When** they save the change, **Then** their account identity is
   updated and reflected consistently going forward.

---

### User Story 2 - A new account gets a fun random name automatically (Priority: P2)

When an account is created, it is automatically given a display name —
generated by combining a random adjective with a random animal name (e.g.
"SwiftFox") — without requiring the player to choose one themselves. The
player can still change it at any time via User Story 1.

**Why this priority**: Removes friction from account creation (no forced
naming step) and gives every new account a friendly, presentable identity
from the start; sequenced after the core view/change capability (User Story
1) since this is about the starting value that capability then operates on.

**Independent Test**: Create a new account without specifying any display
name, and confirm it is immediately assigned a display name combining an
adjective and an animal name, which the player can then view and change
through the normal settings flow.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a new account is created, **When** account creation completes,
   **Then** it already has a display name combining a randomly chosen
   adjective and a randomly chosen animal name, with no naming step required
   from the player.
2. **Given** an account has its automatically generated default display
   name, **When** the player views their settings, **Then** they see that
   generated name exactly as they would see any other display name.
3. **Given** an account still has its automatically generated default
   display name, **When** the player changes it via User Story 1, **Then**
   the change succeeds the same way as changing any other display name.

---

### User Story 3 - Sign out without losing my current game (Priority: P1)

A signed-in player can sign out of their account from settings. Doing so
ends their signed-in session on that device, but does not remove them from a
room or game they're currently in.

**Why this priority**: A fundamental, expected account action; equally
important to display-name management since both are baseline settings
capabilities. Correctness here (not disrupting an active game) matters
enough to be foundational rather than incidental.

**Independent Test**: While signed in and in the middle of an active game,
sign out from settings, and confirm the active game session continues
unaffected (as a guest-equivalent participant) while the account-level
sign-in state is gone.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a signed-in player chooses to sign out, **When** they confirm,
   **Then** their device's signed-in session ends and they return to
   guest-equivalent status.
2. **Given** a player signs out while in an active room or game, **When**
   sign-out completes, **Then** they remain in that room/game exactly as
   before, unaffected by the sign-out.

---

### User Story 4 - Delete my account deliberately (Priority: P2)

A signed-in player can permanently delete their account — removing their
persistent identity, stats, friends list, and saved games — but only through
a deliberate action that isn't easy to trigger by accident, given it cannot
be undone.

**Why this priority**: An important but rarer action than the first two;
correctness (irreversibility being clearly communicated, and the action
being hard to trigger accidentally) matters more than making it fast or
frictionless, which is why this is P2 rather than P1.

**Independent Test**: As a signed-in player with existing stats, friends, and
a saved game, attempt to delete the account, confirm an accidental single
click cannot complete the deletion, then deliberately confirm it and verify
the account's stats, friends list, and saved games are all gone and the
account can no longer be signed into.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a signed-in player selects the delete-account action, **When**
   they have not yet completed a deliberate confirmation step, **Then** the
   account is not deleted.
2. **Given** a signed-in player deliberately confirms account deletion,
   **When** the deletion completes, **Then** their persistent identity,
   stats, friends list, and saved games are all permanently removed.
3. **Given** an account has been deleted, **When** anyone attempts to sign
   into it again, **Then** it can no longer be signed into.

---

### Edge Cases

- What happens if a player attempts to change their display name to one
  that's identical to their current name, or to an empty/invalid value? -> show an error message.
- What happens to a friend's friends-list entry referencing a player who has
  since deleted their account — is that entry simply removed, or shown as a
  removed/former player? -> Simply removed.
- What happens if a player signs out while a forced trade-in or other
  mid-turn requirement is pending in their active game — does that pending
  requirement carry over unaffected, since sign-out doesn't touch the game
  itself? -> Like in the case of a disconnect, if the turn timer ends, we auto-resolve those forced actions.
- What happens if the random name generator produces a combination already
  in use by another account — is a name collision prevented, or are
  duplicate display names across different accounts acceptable? Duplicates
  are acceptable; no global uniqueness constraint is imposed, consistent
  with display names elsewhere in the product not being required to be
  unique.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: System MUST let a signed-in player view their current account
  display name.
- **FR-002**: System MUST let a signed-in player change their account
  display name, with the change reflected consistently everywhere their
  account identity is shown afterward.
- **FR-003**: System MUST let a signed-in player sign out, ending their
  signed-in session on that device.
- **FR-004**: System MUST NOT remove a player from any room or game they are
  currently in as a side effect of signing out.
- **FR-005**: System MUST let a signed-in player permanently delete their
  account, removing their persistent identity, stats, friends list, and
  saved games.
- **FR-006**: System MUST require a deliberate confirmation step, distinct
  from the initial request, before completing account deletion — a single,
  easily accidental action MUST NOT be sufficient to delete an account.
- **FR-007**: System MUST NOT allow a deleted account to be signed into
  again.
- **FR-008**: System MUST automatically assign a display name to a newly
  created account, without requiring the player to choose one.
- **FR-009**: The automatically assigned default display name MUST combine
  a randomly chosen adjective with a randomly chosen animal name (e.g.
  "SwiftFox").
- **FR-010**: System MUST let a player change their automatically assigned
  default display name at any time, through the same display-name-change
  capability used for any other display name (FR-002).
- **FR-011**: System MUST NOT require the generated default display name to
  be unique across accounts — duplicate display names between different
  accounts are acceptable.

### Key Entities

- **Account Settings View**: The signed-in player's own view of their
  account display name and available account actions (sign out, delete
  account).
- **Account Deletion Confirmation**: A distinct, deliberate confirmation step
  required before an account deletion request takes effect.
- **Generated Default Display Name**: The adjective-plus-animal-name display
  name automatically assigned to a new account at creation, changeable
  afterward like any other display name.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: 100% of display name changes are reflected consistently
  wherever a player's account identity is subsequently shown.
- **SC-002**: 100% of sign-outs leave any currently active room/game
  unaffected for that player.
- **SC-003**: 0% of account deletions complete without a distinct,
  deliberate confirmation step beyond the initial request.
- **SC-004**: 100% of deleted accounts have their stats, friends list, and
  saved games fully removed, and can never be signed into again.
- **SC-005**: 100% of newly created accounts have a display name already
  assigned (combining an adjective and an animal name) at the moment
  creation completes, with zero required naming input from the player.

## Assumptions

- This feature depends on the Account System feature (006) for the
  underlying identity, session, stats, friends list, and saved-game data it
  displays and manages; it does not define authentication or the account
  data model itself.
- The specific word lists used for the adjective/animal name generator
  (which adjectives, which animals) are a content/data decision for
  planning, not a scope decision here — the requirement is only the
  adjective-plus-animal-name format and that no player input is required to
  produce one.
- Notification or default-game-option preferences are mentioned as possible
  future additions to this settings surface but are not specified in detail
  here, since no such preferences currently exist elsewhere in the product —
  this feature's concrete, testable scope is display name, sign-out, and
  account deletion.
- Admin-side account management (viewing/banning other accounts) is a
  separate, already-planned admin feature and is out of scope here — this
  feature is only about a player managing their own account.
- The device-level guest username preference (feature 005) is a distinct
  concept from an account's display name managed here; this feature does not
  change or interact with that guest-side preference.
